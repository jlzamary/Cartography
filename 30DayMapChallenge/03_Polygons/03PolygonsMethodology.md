## #30DayMapChallenge Day 3: Polygons

Wanted to keep this one simple and have some fun with the design! When I think of New York City, one of the first things that comes to mind is Spider-Man, specifically the 2018 film Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse. In the iconic “leap of faith” scene, Miles Morales dives off a skyscraper and falls toward the city with the skyline flipped upside down. I tried to capture that feeling here without taking too much away from the map itself.
