## #30DayMapChallenge Day 6: Vintage

For the vintage theme, I knew right away I wanted to try one of John Nelson’s YouTube tutorials I’d been eyeing for a while. I pulled an 1894 map of Lassen Peak, CA from the USGS Historical Topographic Map Collection and draped it over a modern DEM to bring the old survey to life with some terrain.

Getting the DEM aligned and dialing in the hillshade took some trial and error, but for about an hour and a half of work, I’m pretty happy with how it turned out. Next time I’d pick an area with more dramatic relief to really push that 3D effect.
